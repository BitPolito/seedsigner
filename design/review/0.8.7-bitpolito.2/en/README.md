## Colour review

The primary blue follows the first published BitPolito release,
tag `bitpolito_v1`, commit `7ae94fd1005724a971ae897777eb6fa741f3d55d`.

The background is `#F9F9F9`. Every control uses the same focus rule, including
the main menu, dice input, keyboards and lists:

- Unselected: pale `#E8ECFF` surface with primary-blue text and icons.
- Selected: primary `#001CE0` surface with off-white `#F9F9F9` text and icons.
- Disabled: lighter `#F3F5FF` surface with muted text.

This adapts official SeedSigner 0.8.7's hierarchy to a light theme: only the
selected control has a saturated fill. `#A7B2FF` is a secondary decorative
blue, used for keyboard borders; `#6666FF` remains a decorative accent.
Pale blues are not used as small text on the light background.

Descriptive labels, metadata and information messages reuse primary blue
`#001CE0`. Disabled controls reuse the existing `#6666FF` accent.
Success uses muted sage `#4F765E`, warning uses desaturated orange `#956437`,
and errors/critical warnings use dusty rose `#A95360`. These status foregrounds
retain at least 4.5:1 contrast against the page background while softening the
previous saturated palette. Splash and partner-logo backgrounds blend into
the theme at render time; original PNG assets are preserved. QR and camera
output retain the official rendering, including pure white QR backgrounds.


## Bitcoin symbol

Bitcoin amount icons use the supplied [bitsymbol.svg](../../../source-assets/bitsymbol.svg).
Its paths replace only `U+E91D` in the existing SeedSigner icon font; all other
glyphs and the Bitcoin advance width remain unchanged. The loading animation
uses the same glyph. Amounts, denominations and network-indicator colors retain
their original behavior. The SVG is a development source, with no SVG renderer
required on the device.

Regenerate with `python scripts/build-brand-icons.py` after installing
`tests/requirements.txt`; `--check` verifies the generated font. Source and font
SHA-256 values are recorded in `design/bitpolito-theme.json`.

## Live-only surfaces

A few upstream screens intentionally cannot be rendered by the screenshot
generator because they depend on a continuously running camera or QR thread.
They are not missing from the application:

| Surface | Verification |
| --- | --- |
| ScanScreen camera preview | Simulator scan smoke with the deterministic QR fixture; physical camera test |
| QRDisplayScreen signed PSBT / exported xpub | QR brightness-tip screenshot plus simulator QR output smoke; physical display test |
| ToolsImageEntropyLivePreviewScreen | Simulator camera adapter and physical camera test |
| ToolsImageEntropyFinalImageScreen | Simulator camera adapter and physical camera test |
| SeedTranscribeSeedQRConfirmScanView | Simulator/physical SeedQR scan test |

These surfaces are data or hardware driven and must retain the official
white/black QR rendering and camera orientation. They are covered by runtime
smokes rather than static design sheets, so the review set remains
deterministic and portable.

## Approval checklist

Review the complete sheets at 240x240 first. The release gate additionally
smoke-tests ST7789 320x240 and ILI9341 320x240 for startup, Settings, scan and
screensaver. Confirm at least:

- splash, version and partner-logo toggle;
- main menu, power, navigation and scrolling;
- keyboard normal/selected/disabled states;
- passphrase and seed entry;
- Settings, display configuration, camera rotation and inverted colours;
- info, success, warning, error and dire-warning toasts;
- PSBT review, signing and output QR;
- QR display and camera scan (QR modules remain black/white);
- deterministic 30-second screensaver frame;
- no overflow or clipped text.

Screenshots for all locales are generated in CI as artefacts. This checked-in
design review is English-only by decision; translations remain in the official
submodule and are still tested.
